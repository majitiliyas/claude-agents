## Role map (as taught 2026-11-04)
- Data Analyst: answers business questions with data (SQL, spreadsheets, stats, dashboards).
- Product Analyst: same, focused on product (funnels, retention, A/B tests). Closest stepping stone to PM.
- PM: decides what to build and why; priorities, metrics, works with engineering/design.
- PMM: positioning, launch, messaging, go-to-market. "Promote the product at Apple" = PMM.
- Product Engineer: an engineer with product sense who codes daily. Not their path now.
- Route: Analyst → PM/PMM is common. Entry-level PM at big tech is rare. New-grad programs exist (e.g. Google APM and APMM: 2-year rotational, very competitive; the APMM posting seen said no immigration sponsorship. Check official pages).

## Trial A spec: "Стипендия" (now a PRODUCT project)
From the founder's pain: making a 50,000 KZT/month stipend last.
- MVP IN: budget, medicine reserve, logging like `1200 еда`, "safe to spend today" = (budget − reserve − spent) / days left, weekly summary. Categories: еда, лекарства, такси, развлечения.
- Build options: AI-assisted Telegram bot, teammate-built bot, or Google Form → Sheet as the fastest MVP. Choose the one that gets 5 users soonest.
- Metrics: D7 retention, entries per active user per day, % users who check "safe to spend".
- Success: 5 classmates each log ≥3 expenses across 2 days; founder uses it 2+ weeks; dashboard + insight memo shipped.
